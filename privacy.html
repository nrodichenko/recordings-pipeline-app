Privacy policy

Recordings pipeline · last updated 24 November 2026

Recordings pipeline is a personal tool. Its only user is the person who set it up, and it only accesses that person's own Google account.

What Google data it accesses

Files that the user puts into the Google Drive folder "Recordings inbox". The app lists that folder, downloads the audio files in it, and moves each file to the Drive trash once it has been downloaded. It does not open, change or share any other Drive file, and it never deletes files permanently.

Where the data goes

Downloaded recordings are stored and transcribed on the user's own Mac. To give a recording a short title, the text of its transcript may be sent to the user's own Anthropic (Claude) account. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising, and no data is sent to the developer.

Keeping and removing data

Recordings and transcripts stay on the user's Mac until the user deletes them. Access to Google Drive can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
