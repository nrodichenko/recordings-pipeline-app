Recordings pipeline

A personal tool, used by its owner only. It is not offered to anyone else.

Recordings pipeline runs on its owner's Mac. It picks up audio recordings that the owner drops into one Google Drive folder ("Recordings inbox"), downloads them to the Mac for transcription, and then moves the Drive copy to the Drive trash.

It uses the Google Drive API only for that one folder: listing it, downloading the files in it, and moving them to the trash. It does not read, change or share any other file.
